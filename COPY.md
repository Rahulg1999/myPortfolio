# Copy rules

How every line on this site is written. Taken from five companies in the same
niche — offline-first, field-grade mobile software — and cut down to what a
one-person portfolio can use.

| Company | What they sell | The pattern worth stealing |
|---|---|---|
| **Very Good Ventures** | Flutter consultancy | Parallel fragments: *"Build once. Deploy everywhere."* |
| **PowerSync** | Offline-first sync for SQLite | Name the pain in plain words first: *"Escape the network tarpit."* |
| **Ditto** | Edge / peer-to-peer sync | Outcome before technology: *"Servers & Cloud Optional."* |
| **Mitti** (SafetyCulture) | Inspections for frontline teams | Every number carries its noun: *"380K+ inspections daily."* |
| **Fieldwire** | Jobsite management | Field-worker verbs, proof before the ask: *"Real work, not paperwork."* |

## The rules

1. **One idea per screen.** Each screen has one headline that stands on its
   own. The body proves that headline and nothing else. If a sentence needs
   "and also", it belongs on another screen.
2. **Headlines are 3–8 words.** Full stop at the end. No colons, no dashes.
3. **Outcome first, technology second.** Say what the person on site gets
   ("Nothing is lost."), then how ("a sync queue that survives a force-quit").
   *(Ditto, Mitti)*
4. **Name the place, not the problem category.** Basements, plant rooms,
   warehouses. Not "low-connectivity environments". *(PowerSync, Fieldwire)*
5. **Every number has a noun and a source.** "10 major versions of one app",
   not "extensive experience". If the number is the firm's and not mine, it
   does not go on the page. *(Mitti, Fieldwire)*
6. **Lists are parallel fragments.** Same grammar, same length:
   "PIN. Geofence. Biometrics." *(VGV, Fieldwire)*
7. **Proof comes before the ask.** Store listings and real screens sit above
   the contact block. *(Fieldwire, Mitti)*
8. **Buttons say what happens.** "Email me", "Download résumé (PDF)",
   "Open RahulOS". Never "Learn more", "Explore", "Discover".
9. **Say what is not finished.** Unreleased and pulled apps say so, in the same
   sentence as what they do.
10. **First person, plain verbs.** I built, I shipped, it syncs, it saves.
    Past tense for shipped work, present tense for what runs today.

## Words that do not go on this site

These read as generated. If one appears, rewrite the sentence, do not swap
in a synonym.

> leverage · seamless · robust · cutting-edge · innovative · passionate ·
> elevate · empower · unlock · harness · delve · journey · transform ·
> revolutionize · synergy · world-class · dynamic · game-changer ·
> next-level · effortless · streamline · holistic · supercharge · crafted ·
> meticulous · bespoke · tapestry · landscape · realm · ecosystem ·
> "not just X, but Y" · "in today's fast-paced world" ·
> "at the intersection of" · "I'm passionate about"

Also out:

- **Em dashes in headlines.** Use a full stop. In body copy, one per screen at most.
- **Three adjectives in a row.** Pick the one that is true.
- **Rhetorical questions.** The one exception is the contact line.
- **Hedging filler.** "really", "truly", "very", "quite", "actually".

## Check before shipping a line

- Can a site supervisor read it aloud without sounding like a brochure?
- Is it true, and could I point at the build, the listing or the commit?
- Would this screen still make sense with everything else deleted?
