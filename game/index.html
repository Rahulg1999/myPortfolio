Threats & systems
  • Extraction opens at wave 5 and never closes. Leaving pays out immediately — but clearing wave 10 and then extracting adds a +1,500 cr deep-run bonus and a chunk of XP. Go down before you reach the pad and you lose both.
  • Bosses hold every fifth wave. A Warden at wave 5, then a Sovereign at wave 10 — and they alternate from there. Cores sit behind armour on both: the hull takes nothing until every core is gone.
  • The Sovereign is the harder fight. Five cores instead of three, a much heavier hull, and a second phase — below half health it overdrives into faster volleys, a five-wide fan, and starts calling escorts. It is the reason to stay past the pad.
  • Wave 6 is a breather by design. The pressure eases straight after the Warden so you can spend what dropped, then climbs again to the wave-10 fight.
  • Drums chain-detonate. Kite a pack past one and put a round through it.
  • District events hit every third wave, drawn at random and never the same one twice running. Grid failure kills the power outright — every window and neon sign goes out, and hostile shells running hot are all you get. Smog bank collapses visibility but extends the radar. Power surge makes everything faster and pays more. Grav flux, supply cache and ion wind are gifts, not hazards — the banner tells you which you got.
  • Mods are global. Salvage one and every weapon you own inherits it.

Playing bots on your own? You do not need the server at all — pick a mode, choose how many bots, and press Start match.

To play with other people: run node server.js next to the game file on one machine, then open the address it prints. Everyone else on the same wifi opens that same address in their browser — no install needed on their end.

The host sets the match. Arena, bots, frag limit and time limit all travel with the match, so everyone drops into the same map on the same layout. A match ends on whichever cap comes first, then the scoreboard shows kills, deaths and assists before returning here. If the host abandons, the match ends for everyone.

Team deathmatch splits by join order and spawns each side together at opposite ends. Your team shows on the radar as arrows pointing the way each player is facing.
Connected 1 player
Multiplayer is PvP only: Duel, Deathmatch and Team Deathmatch. Positions, hits, kills, scores and respawns all sync between machines.

Inside a match the Armoury is switched off — permanent upgrades are ignored and every weapon is issued to everyone, so matches are decided by aim rather than by who has ground more credits.

For solo PvE waves, use Deploy from the main menu.
