Lead developer · v10.0.0

Problem

A local-first architecture — every form, pin and photo written to the device immediately, then drained by a background sync engine that survives the app being killed and resolves conflicts on the way up.

Lead developer: architecture, the sync engine, dynamic forms, on-image annotation, QR scanning, in-app PDF reports, release.
