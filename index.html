HRM OTM

2 live listings · ships as ClockedIn HRM

Flutter · multi-tenant HR, leave and attendance · feature developer

The problem. Two clients wanted the same HR app with different branding, rules and document storage — without paying for two codebases.

What I did. Leave and balance tracking, clock-in/out and reporting, OAuth2 login that injects the tenant header from the cached company record, and the two-flavour release setup — separate application IDs and signing per client from one codebase. I also built the face-attendance path: Face attendance runs entirely on the handset — MobileFaceNet embeddings and a MiniFASNetV2-SE anti-spoof classifier, so no face image leaves the phone, with a session policy that coaches framing and head tilt rather than failing people, covered by unit tests. Live on both stores as ClockedIn HRM.

How I work

The habits behind the six
01 The device is the source of truth

I design the local database first and treat the network as a background job that expects to fail. It is slower to build and it is the reason none of these apps lose a day's work.

02 Every write says who said so

Compliance and payroll get argued about later. Attribution locks on first touch, edits are recorded with their author, and the app refuses to imply data it does not have.

03 Built for the worst phone on site

Mid-range Android in a basement, not a flagship on wifi. Lists come from the device, images are compressed before upload, and the sync queue survives the app being killed.

04 AI only where it pays rent

Gemini earns a place when it saves the team real hours — drafting test cases and docs — and stays out of the way when a plain form would do the job better.
